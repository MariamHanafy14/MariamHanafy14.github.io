Power BI Dashboard

Udemy Courses Performance Analysis

An interactive Power BI dashboard that turns raw Udemy course data into actionable insight — helping course creators, learners, and platform managers make better decisions on pricing, engagement, and content strategy.

Power BI Excel DAX

The Problem & Key Questions

E-learning platforms host thousands of courses, but stakeholders often struggle to identify what actually drives success. This dashboard answers:

  • How much revenue is generated by month, and which periods perform best?
  • How are students distributed across months and lecture segments?
  • What is the course distribution across levels — Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, All Levels?
  • How do free vs. paid courses compare in availability and popularity?
  • What are the most popular courses by number of students and reviews?

The Dashboard

  • KPIs overview: number of courses, average price, total revenue, students, and engagement rate.
  • Revenue analysis: total revenue by month, highlighting seasonal trends.
  • Student trends: student counts by month and lecture segment.
  • Course distribution: donut charts for free vs. paid and course level.
  • Top 5 courses: ranked by students and reviews.

A dynamic X-axis lets users switch a single visual between Subject, Level, Price Segment, Lecture Segment, Duration, and Payment Status, with drill-downs on the donut and line charts.

Key Insights

  • Seasonal revenue peaks appear around March, August, and November.
  • Low engagement at scale: a 9M-strong student base has only a 5% engagement rate, leaving room to improve.
  • Accessibility focus: 52% of courses target "All Levels," while only 12% are expert-level.
  • Paid dominates: paid courses make up 92% of the platform.
  • Web development courses consistently attract the most students and reviews.
  • Sweet spot length: courses with 20–50 lectures attract the largest student base.

Recommendations

Boost engagement

Introduce gamification, quizzes, or community discussions to lift engagement beyond 5%.

Promote expert-level courses

Encourage more advanced content to serve learners seeking specialization and career growth.

Expand free offerings

Add more free entry-point courses to onboard students and funnel them into paid learning paths.

Leverage seasonal trends

Launch marketing campaigns during peak months — March, August, and November — to maximize revenue.
