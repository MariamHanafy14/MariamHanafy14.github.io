Medical Sales Performance Dashboard

A clear, easy-to-use Power BI dashboard built for a medical company to track sales, measure sales-rep performance against targets, and surface the insights that help the business grow. This was my first end-to-end data project, from cleaning the raw data to shipping the final interactive report.

Power BI Excel Power Query DAX Galaxy Schema
1 / 3

The Problem

The management team needed a better way to see sales performance than complex spreadsheets. They wanted one simple dashboard that could answer key questions quickly:

  • Are we hitting our sales targets for the year?
  • Who are our best sales reps, and who needs a little more support?
  • Which products and customer groups are generating the most revenue?
  • Are there important sales patterns over time we should know about?

The Dashboard

The final report has three focused, easy-to-navigate pages:

  • Sales Analysis: a quick look at Total Sales, growth, and performance by month, product, and customer group.
  • Target Analysis: compares actual sales to targets for every sales rep.
  • Contribution Analysis: uses a Decomposition Tree to dig into the details behind the numbers.

Cross-filtering on click, filters for Year / Month / Rep, and hover tooltips make it fast to explore and easy to trust.

How I Built It

Excel — data cleaning Power Query — transformation Galaxy Schema modeling DAX — Target Achievement %

The data model connects two main tables — sales and targets — using a Galaxy Schema, which was the best way to keep the comparisons between actuals and targets accurate.

Key Insights

  • Almost there: the team hit 96.9% of its yearly target, leaving a small 3.1% gap to close.
  • A star performer: Sara was the only rep to beat her target, reaching 102.3%.
  • The main money-maker: the "Chain" customer group brings in most of the sales.
  • The hit product: GLUCOPHAGE is the best-seller by far — a strength, but also a concentration risk.
  • Room to improve: the gap between Sara and the rest of the team shows a clear coaching opportunity.

Recommendations

Learn from the best

Have Sara lead a session sharing her winning sales tips so the rest of the team can adopt what works.

Don't put all eggs in one basket

Launch a small campaign to grow sales in other customer groups, like Clinic and Semi, to reduce dependency on Chain.

Sell more, sell smarter

Bundle GLUCOPHAGE with other products to lift cross-sell revenue and reduce reliance on a single hit product.

Help everyone succeed

Use the dashboard in one-on-one meetings with reps to identify problem areas and build a support plan together.
