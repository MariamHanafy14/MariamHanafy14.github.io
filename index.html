About Me

I’m a Data Analyst with a background in Decision Support & Operations Research from the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence, Cairo University. I work with data to uncover patterns, measure performance, and turn raw information into clear insights that support better business decisions. I have hands-on experience working with real business data across different projects and companies. At Almia Chem, I worked with ERP data to analyze sales and purchasing activities, track sales representatives’ performance against targets, and build recurring reports and dashboards using Excel and Power BI. I also worked with Reef Group on Power BI reporting, validating measures and KPI logic within a Microsoft Fabric semantic model. My technical toolkit includes Power BI, SQL, Excel, Python, and Microsoft Fabric, with experience in data cleaning, analysis, data modeling, KPI development, dashboard building, and extracting actionable insights from business data. Beyond analysis, I enjoy sharing knowledge and working with others. I’ve worked as a Data Analysis Instructor at Flashat and led the Data Analysis Committee at Star Union, where I taught Excel and Power BI, mentored members, and supported data-focused projects. I’m interested in solving real business problems through data and building analytical solutions that are clear, practical, and useful for decision-making.
