About Me

I've always been drawn to the stories hidden inside data — because behind every number, there's a decision waiting to be made. As a senior Computer Science student at Cairo University (FCAI), I've spent the past few years turning that curiosity into real skills: building dashboards in Power BI, writing SQL queries, and transforming raw, messy data into insights that actually matter.

I've had the chance to apply this in a real work environment at Almia Chem, where I analyzed ERP data, tracked sales performance, and built reports that supported day-to-day business decisions. Beyond the technical side, I genuinely enjoy sharing knowledge — which is why I've worked as a Data Analysis Instructor at Flashat, and currently lead the Data Analysis Committee at Star Union, mentoring members and driving data projects within the organization.

I'm always looking for opportunities to grow, take on new challenges, and work on projects where data can solve real problems and drive smarter decisions.
